About Me

I’m currently pursuing a Master’s in Information Design and Data Visualisation at MIT Institute of Design, MIT-ADT University. My academic background is an unconventional mix of Culinary Arts and Design.

I began my design journey as a visual designer and food journalist, working across photography, graphic editing, and visual storytelling. As my career evolved, I pursued a Diploma in Web Design and Development, which introduced me to digital products and UI/UX.

B e y o n d D e s i g n

Growing up, I was often called a “jack of all trades” always curious to try something new.

From lawn tennis, shooting, public speaking, and volunteering to training in Indian classical instruments and dance, I’ve always enjoyed exploring unfamiliar spaces.

I’m also an avid reader, with books being my true ethereal love. That curiosity continues to drive me to explore, learn, and discover.
